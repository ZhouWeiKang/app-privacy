Privacy policy

How Arrow Space - 3DPuzzle handles information.

Arrow Space - 3DPuzzle is provided by Weikang Zhou. This policy describes information processed when you use the game and when you contact player support.

Information stored on your device

The game stores level progress, Arrow King records, local rewards, audio and vibration preferences, and your selected language on your device. The game does not currently require an account or provide a cloud-save service. Arrow King uses a locally simulated opponent.

Gameplay analytics

The game integrates Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand use and improve gameplay. Our gameplay events include level and match starts and outcomes, completion time, remaining arrows, hints, revives, scores, and rank. Firebase may also process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and automatically collected usage information as described in its documentation.

Learn more: Firebase privacy and security and Google privacy policy.

Advertising

The game uses Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads to provide rewarded video and interstitial advertisements. Advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers where available, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from network information, and ad interaction or performance information. On iOS, the game asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before advertising identifiers are used for personalized advertising; if you decline, ads are requested without tracking permission. Processing also depends on the service, device permissions, and applicable privacy choices. Rewarded ads are optional. An interstitial may appear between completed levels.

Learn more about the advertising providers' practices and choices: Unity privacy policy and Unity Ads privacy information.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases, including removing ads, item bundles, and coin packs. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive your payment card details. The game stores purchased entitlements and coin balances locally on your device. Apple may provide us with purchase history or transaction information for support, refunds, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Restoring purchases uses Apple's StoreKit and may require you to authenticate with your Apple ID.

Support requests

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information.

Sharing, retention, and security

Analytics and advertising information is processed by the service providers described above for the relevant purposes. Those providers may process information outside your country. Their policies explain their processing locations, retention, and security practices. Local game progress remains on your device unless you remove it or the app. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and related service or legal obligations.

Your choices and requests

You can manage app permissions and any available tracking preferences in iOS Settings. Removing the app removes its local game data, but does not by itself remove information already processed by third-party providers. Contact us about access, correction, or deletion requests; the options available depend on applicable law and our ability to identify the relevant records. Provider privacy links above describe additional choices.

Children

The game does not include player chat or public profiles. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request or another interaction with us, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
