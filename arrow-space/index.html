Common questions

How do I clear an arrow?

Drag to rotate the 3D model and inspect its faces. Tap an arrow when the path in its direction is clear. A blocked arrow costs a life. You can zoom in or use a hint to find the next move.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings, choose a language from the dropdown, then tap Confirm. The game supports 16 languages. Choose Follow system to use your iPhone's language; unsupported languages use English. Your manual choice is remembered on this device.

Where is my progress saved?

Level progress, rank progress, and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not currently provide an account or cloud-save service. Deleting the app or replacing your device may remove your progress.

Is Arrow King an online mode?

Arrow King pits you against a locally simulated opponent on the same 3D model. The opponent is a bot. Your total wins and rank are separate from your main level progress.

I watched an ad but did not receive a reward.

A rewarded video must complete successfully. Skipping the ad does not grant a reward. If no ad is ready, check your internet connection and try again later. If the problem continues, contact support with the time it happened and the reward you expected.

The app freezes or closes unexpectedly.

Close and reopen the app, check for updates, and restart your iPhone if necessary. Contact support if the issue repeats. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting if you want to preserve local progress.
