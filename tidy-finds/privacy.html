Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Weikang Zhou handles information when you play Tidy Finds: Sorting Puzzle on iOS or Android or contact support.

Game progress and settings

You can play without creating an account. Your level progress, stars, best times, energy, tutorial progress, and language, sound, and vibration settings are saved on your device. We use this local save to restore your game progress and preferences when you return to the game.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob to offer rewarded ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. Depending on your platform, region, device settings, and consent choices, Google’s SDKs may process:

  • Your IP address and an approximate location that may be inferred from it.
  • Device or advertising identifiers when available, and device and app information.
  • Ad requests, impressions, taps, video views, reward events, and other app interactions.
  • Crash and performance data and signals about your privacy choices.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, evaluate ad performance, diagnose technical issues, and detect invalid ad traffic. Where permitted by your choices and device settings, data may also be used to personalize ads or measure them across apps and websites.

We require third parties that receive personal information from us to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements. We use AdMob under Google’s applicable data protection terms. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google also explains its safeguards for international advertising data transfers.

Manage your choices

When Privacy choices appears in the game’s settings, open it to review or change your advertising consent choices. You can also use the advertising privacy controls on your device.

On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt controls permission to track activity across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you see this prompt, you can allow or decline tracking, and change your choice under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can keep playing if you decline.

Support messages and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use it to respond, investigate issues, and handle requests. We keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain relevant records, and meet legal obligations.

Your game data stays in the game’s local storage until you clear the app’s data where that option is available or delete the app. Device backups are managed by your operating system and may retain copies. Google handles retention of information it processes under its own policy.

These support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process information needed to serve these pages under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may be able to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, or withdraw consent. For information held by Google, you can also use the controls and contact options described in its privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us so we can review your request.

Changes and contact

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Weikang Zhou at 310823243@qq.com. For help with the game, visit the support page.
