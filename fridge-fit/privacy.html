Privacy policy

How Fridge Fit: Food Puzzle handles information.

Fridge Fit: Food Puzzle is provided by Weikang Zhou. This policy describes information processed when you use the game and when you contact player support.

Information stored on your device

The game stores level progress, coins, unlocked skins, audio and vibration preferences, and your selected language on your device. The game does not require an account and does not provide a cloud-save service.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases: skip-ticket packs and a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive your payment card details. Ticket balances and the Remove Ads entitlement are stored locally on your device. Apple may provide us with purchase history or transaction information for support, refunds, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Restoring purchases uses Apple's StoreKit and may require you to authenticate with your Apple ID. Tickets are consumable and are not restored; Remove Ads is non-consumable and can be restored.

Advertising

The game uses Unity LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads to show optional rewarded video ads. When you choose to watch an ad, Unity and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. If you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, ads may be personalized; if you decline, ads are non-personalized. You can skip a rewarded ad by using a skip ticket, or remove ads entirely with the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Learn more in the Unity privacy policy and Unity game player privacy policy.

Analytics

The game uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to understand how players use the game and to improve it. Firebase Analytics processes gameplay events (such as level start and end, in-app purchases, and language changes), an app instance identifier, and device and app information such as device model, operating-system version, and app version. We use this information for analytics and product improvement. Device identifiers may also be used for advertising and tracking as described below. The game requests App Tracking Transparency permission before using data for personalized advertising.

Firebase may process information outside your country. Learn more: Firebase privacy and security and Google privacy policy.

Support requests

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. Analytics information is processed by Google as described above. Local game progress remains on your device unless you remove it or the app. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and related service or legal obligations.

Your choices

You can manage app permissions in iOS Settings. Removing the app removes its local game data. Contact us about access, correction, or deletion requests; the options available depend on applicable law and our ability to identify the relevant records.

Children

The game does not include player chat or public profiles and does not collect personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
