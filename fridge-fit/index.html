Common questions

How do I play?

Open the fridge, pick food from the basket, and pack every item onto the shelves and into the drawers. Space is limited, so plan the layout and make it all fit. Tap and drag to place items.

Where is my progress saved?

Level progress, coins, unlocked skins, and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not require an account and does not provide cloud save. Deleting the app may remove your progress.

I bought coins or skins but did not receive them.

Make sure the purchase completed in the App Store. Reopen the game and use the restore purchases option in the store screen. If the items still do not appear, contact support with the approximate purchase time and the product name. Do not send your Apple ID password or payment details.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the in-game store and choose Restore Purchases. Non-consumable purchases such as the all-skins unlock can be restored on the same Apple ID. Consumable coin packs are added to your balance when purchased and are not restorable.

The app freezes or closes unexpectedly.

Close and reopen the app, make sure you have the latest version, and restart your iPhone if needed. If the issue repeats, contact support with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.
