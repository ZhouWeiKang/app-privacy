Privacy policy

How Sand Workshop handles information and your choices.

Sand Workshop is provided by Weikang Zhou. This policy covers information processed by the game, its advertising and analytics providers, and player support. The game does not require an account.

Local game information

Level progress, coins, boosters, ad-ticket balances, unlocks, purchase entitlements, transaction identifiers, language, audio and vibration settings are stored on your device. We do not operate a game-account or cloud-save service. Deleting the app or replacing the device may remove local progress and consumables.

In-app purchases

Apple processes payments; we do not receive card details. The game uses StoreKit receipts and transaction identifiers to validate purchases, deliver items once and restore the non-consumable Remove Full-Screen Ads entitlement. Coins, boosters and ad tickets are consumables. Apple may provide transaction information for purchase support, refunds, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

Advertising

The game uses Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads for voluntary rewarded videos and occasional interstitials at level transitions. The SDKs may process device/app identifiers, IP address and IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions, purchase/usage information, performance and diagnostic information to provide, measure, secure and improve advertising. We configure LevelPlay with GDPR consent not granted and restricted CCPA processing. These restrictions do not mean the SDKs process no data. Use of the advertising identifier is subject to Apple tracking permission. Declining permission does not prevent gameplay.

Remove Full-Screen Ads removes interstitials; voluntary rewarded videos remain. One Ad Ticket replaces one voluntary rewarded video. Advertising-provider privacy controls are accessible through the privacy icon on an ad. Manage Apple tracking permission in iOS Settings. See Unity game player privacy policy for provider practices, retention and rights.

Optional usage analytics

Firebase Analytics, a Google service, is disabled until you opt in through Settings > Data choices. If enabled, it processes gameplay and purchase-related events, app-instance/device identifiers, app version and device/OS information to understand usage and improve the game. We do not supply a name, email address or game-account ID to Firebase. You may withdraw permission in Data choices at any time, stopping future analytics collection. Apple tracking permission is separate from this choice.

Providers may process information outside your country. See Firebase privacy and security and Google privacy policy.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as a problem description, device details or screenshot. This is used to respond and investigate. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. This website does not embed advertising or analytics scripts.

Sharing, retention and transfers

Advertising and analytics information is processed by Unity and Google as described above, including outside your country. Provider retention follows their policies and applicable service settings. Local saves remain on your device until removed. We retain support correspondence only as needed for the request and related service or legal obligations. We do not sell support correspondence. Advertising providers may process identifiers and advertising information subject to the restrictions and choices described above.

Your choices and requests

Manage optional analytics in the game at Settings > Data choices, tracking in iOS Settings, and provider choices through an ad privacy icon. Contact us for access, correction or deletion of information we hold, subject to applicable law and our ability to locate it. Provider-specific rights may also be exercised through Unity and Google. Removing the app deletes local data, but does not automatically delete Apple or provider records. Opting out of analytics affects future collection and is not itself a request to erase prior records.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. It has no chat, public profiles or user-generated-content sharing. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information to us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes

We will update this page when data practices change. The revision date appears above.
