How do I play?

Move colored pieces to matching sand. Fill each piece to clear it, manage space and work through locks, frozen pieces and layered sand. The home screen also offers Sand Jam, spinning-track puzzles and Super Fun challenges as you progress or unlock access.

Where are purchases?

Open Shop and select Coins & Packs. The store offers three coin packs, a booster bundle, Remove Full-Screen Ads, and 10 Ad Tickets. Prices shown in the game come from the App Store for your region.

What does Remove Full-Screen Ads do?

This one-time purchase removes interstitial ads between levels. Voluntary rewarded videos remain available. It does not grant unlimited ad tickets.

How do Ad Tickets work?

Each ticket replaces one voluntarily selected rewarded video and grants the corresponding reward. A ticket is used automatically when you have one. With no tickets, rewarded videos work normally. The pack contains 10 tickets; your regional App Store price is displayed before purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Shop > Coins & Packs and use Restore Purchases at the bottom. Use the Apple Account used for purchase. Remove Full-Screen Ads is restorable. Coins, boosters and ad tickets are consumables and are not restored after deletion or device replacement. Contact support if a completed purchase did not arrive; Apple handles payment and refunds.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, balances, settings and unlocks are saved locally. No game account or cloud-save service is provided. Deleting the app or replacing your device can remove progress and consumables. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting.

Settings and privacy

Settings provides language, sound, music and vibration controls, plus Privacy Policy, Support and Data choices. The game supports 16 languages. Optional usage analytics is off by default; you can enable or disable it in Data choices. Apple tracking permission is managed separately in iOS Settings.

An ad did not load or give a reward.

Check your connection and try later. Rewarded videos must complete successfully. Skipping or a failed ad does not earn a reward. Contact support with the approximate time, mode, level and expected reward if the issue repeats.

The game is slow or closes unexpectedly.

Close and reopen the game, check for updates and restart the device. Contact us with the device model, mode and level. Avoid reinstallation while troubleshooting to preserve local data.
