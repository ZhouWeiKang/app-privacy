  • Approximate location: advertising providers may infer a general region from your IP address. The App does not request precise GPS location for advertising.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

  • deliver rewarded and interstitial advertisements;
  • select contextual or, where permitted, personalized advertisements;
  • measure ad delivery, frequency, engagement, and campaign performance;
  • provide in-game rewards after eligible rewarded advertisements;
  • detect invalid traffic, abuse, and advertising fraud;
  • diagnose errors, improve stability, and maintain the App; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, and platform requirements.

3. Advertising, Consent, and Tracking

In regions where consent is required, the App displays a consent message before initializing personalized advertising. You may choose non-personalized advertising. Non-personalized ads may still use contextual information, limited identifiers, IP addresses, fraud-prevention data, and frequency controls.

On iOS 14 or later, the App may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the IDFA or allowing tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. Denying ATT permission does not prevent you from using the App, but advertisements may be less relevant and some measurement may be limited.

You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where available, you can also reopen the consent interface through Settings > Privacy Choices inside the App.
